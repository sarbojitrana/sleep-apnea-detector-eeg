## Research sources checked

Four distinct research papers are summarized and cited on the slides:

1. **Alam et al., 2024 — EEG features and FPGA/SVM classification.** Original paper text reviewed through the authors' uploaded manuscript, including feature methodology and conclusion. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3374223), [author-uploaded paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/378758838_Energy-Efficient_FPGA_Based_Sleep_Apnea_Detection_Using_EEG_Signals).
2. **Barnes et al., 2022 — single-channel EEG and an explainable CNN.** Publisher full text reviewed, particularly methods, subject-wise evaluation and generalization limitations. [Publisher full text](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0272167).
3. **Surrel et al., 2018 — online wearable ECG classification.** Authors' accepted manuscript reviewed, including the patient-specific setup. It uses ECG, not EEG. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TBCAS.2018.2824659), [institutional manuscript](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/b33a75d0-fdf4-44dc-a376-303607444f9c/content).
4. **Padovano et al., 2022 — cross-validation versus external validation.** Original conference paper reviewed, including methods, results and conclusions. [Full paper](https://cinc.org/archives/2022/pdf/CinC2022-086.pdf).

The user-suggested **Sara Mohan George** source is included as additional reading. Its expanded title and authorship were verified from the [publisher's chapter listing](https://www.routledge.com/Advances-in-Wearables-and-Nearables-for-Healthcare-Applications/Naik/p/book/9781032796543). The full chapter was not available, so it is not counted among the four reviewed research papers and no experimental claims are attributed to it.

The actual EEG training dataset is separately cited: [MIT-BIH Polysomnographic Database v1.0.0](https://physionet.org/content/slpdb/1.0.0/).

## Accuracy and hardware status

The presentation preserves the measured software results: 74.0% accuracy, 80.2% sensitivity, 70.9% specificity, 58.4% precision and 67.6% F1. The results are presented as a research benchmark, with no claim of clinical validation. The full subject-level breakdown remains in EEG_PERFORMANCE_REPORT.md.

The supplied upstream presentation specifies Chebyshev-II filtering, Haar division-by-two scaling and planned Mean/MAV/Energy/Variance features. The software reference currently uses Butterworth SOS, orthonormal Haar and Energy/Mobility/Kurtosis. Slide 14 identifies the need to agree on a common feature contract, scaling and normalization before hardware integration. No application code or FPGA implementation was changed as part of making this deck.
